Questions

Does NIVANT Connect to My Bank?

No. You add transactions yourself or import a statement file you export from your bank. NIVANT never asks for your bank login.

Where Is My Data Stored?

On your phone. Your accounts, transactions and budgets are never sent to us. The only things that go online are feedback you send, crash reports and your notification token. The privacy policy lists them all.

What Happens If I Change Phone?

Export a backup from Settings and restore it on the new phone.

How Do I Report a Problem or Suggest Something?

Use the Feedback screen in the app. You'll see replies there, and get a notification when there's one.
